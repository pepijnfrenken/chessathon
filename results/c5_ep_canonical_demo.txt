q5-c5 — EP-square canonicalisation: demonstration
=================================================
tool: tools/probe_c5_ep_canonical.py (dev tool, NOT shipped)
date: 2026-09-10

PROBLEM (audit-6 §C5, root cause of codex5 F3)
`parse_fen` and `make_move_apply` disagreed on when the ep square exists
AND is capturable:
  * make_move_apply set it after a double push whenever an enemy pawn
    merely SAT beside the landing square (pseudo-legal, pin-blind);
  * parse_fen kept whatever the FEN text declared, untested.
python-chess — the legality oracle this project validates against — keeps an
ep square only while has_legal_en_passant() holds, i.e. it is PIN-AWARE. So
the same board reached by play and by FEN got two different zobrist keys,
breaking repetition identity and causing a class of TT misses.

FIX (one rule, one implementation)
engine/board.py gains `ep_capturable(st, ep_sq, side)` — the single source of
truth for "the ep square exists and the capture is legal". It verifies (a) a
pawn of the capturing colour stands beside the just-pushed pawn, (b) removing
both pawns and placing the capturer on the ep square does NOT leave the
capturing side's king in check. Both callers now use it:
  * make_move_apply: after a double push, `ep_capturable(st, cand, 1 - side)`
    (the capturing side is the side to move once the push completes);
  * parse_fen: a declared ep square is dropped when `ep_capturable` is false.
King squares are now located BEFORE the canonicalisation, since the helper
needs them.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE (an error caught by the probe, not by reasoning)
The helper's first draft tested candidate capturers on the squares beside
the EP SQUARE rather than beside the pushed PAWN. The probe caught it
immediately (the "free" case then reported the ep square kept as "-" and
d4xe3 as not generated) and the geometry was corrected before the fix was
judged. Recorded here because it is exactly the class of bug the oracle-
first probe exists to catch.
